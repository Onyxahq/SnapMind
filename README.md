## Why You'll Love SnapMind

Still juggling four different apps just to take a screenshot, copy unselectable text, translate a foreign menu, and record a quick clip? **SnapMind replaces all of them with one ultra-light, ridiculously fast tool.**

- **See foreign text in a game, PDF, or error popup?** Draw a box — SnapMind translates it and **places the translation right over the original image**, matching colors and font size so it reads like it was native all along.
- **Need to pull a table out of a screenshot or slide?** Don't retype a single cell — **recognize it in one click and paste straight into Excel or Word** with merged cells preserved.
- **Want to keep a reference image or note in front of your eyes?** **Pin it right on top of your screen** — and yes, you can even highlight and copy text directly from pinned screenshots!
- **Long webpage or quick how-to clip?** **Scrolling capture and screen recording (MP4 / GIF)** are already built right in.

## Download & Install (30 Seconds)

> **👉 [Click here to download the latest version from GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Onyxahq/SnapMind/releases/latest)**

1. Open the **[Releases](https://github.com/Onyxahq/SnapMind/releases/latest)** page and download `SnapMind_<version>_x64-setup.exe` (around **10 MB**).
2. Double-click to install on **Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit)** — install for just yourself or for all users.
3. Launch SnapMind! A quick welcome screen lets you optionally grab the **free offline text-reading pack (~21 MB)** or connect your favorite AI provider. That's it — you're ready to go!

## What SnapMind Can Do for You

### 1. Instant Screenshots & Clean Annotations
- Press **`Shift+F1`** and your screen freezes instantly — even across multiple monitors.
- Automatically snaps to windows and buttons so your captures look crisp and effortless.
- Includes a pixel magnifier & color picker, arrows, boxes, highlighter, text, numbered steps, and sharp mosaic/blur for hiding sensitive info.
- Copy once and paste seamlessly into chat apps, documents, or File Explorer.

### 2. Free Offline Text, Table, Formula & Markdown Reader
- **Works 100% offline**: Read text in English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and more without paying a cent or uploading private screenshots to any server.
- **Pick only the lines you want**: Click individual text boxes on the screenshot to keep or skip specific lines.
- **Tables straight to Excel**: Preview, edit cells inline, copy directly into Excel / Word, or export `.xlsx` and `.csv`.
- **Math formulas & Markdown**: Turn screenshot equations into ready-to-paste LaTeX formulas, or turn articles into clean Markdown notes.

### 3. Smart Translation That Understands Context
- **In-place image translation**: Translates right on top of the screenshot while skipping code, numbers, and formulas automatically.
- **Select-and-translate anywhere (`Ctrl+Alt+D`)**: Highlight any text in any app and get a clean, draggable translation card next to your cursor. Pin the card to keep translating without losing your flow.
- **Bring your favorite AI or translator**: Built-in presets for **ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, DeepL, Google Translate, Microsoft Translator, Qwen, Kimi, Ollama (local AI)**, and 24+ services — plus automatic backup failover if one service is busy.

### 4. Supercharged Floating Pin Windows
- Press **`Ctrl+T`** to pin any screenshot — or **`Shift+F3`** to pin whatever image or text is in your clipboard.
- **Copy text directly off any pinned image** automatically.
- Scroll to zoom, adjust transparency, annotate right on the pin, or enable click-through so it never gets in your way.
- Built-in history remembers closed pins and translations, and restores open pins if Windows restarts unexpectedly.

### 5. Scrolling Screenshots & Quick Screen Recording
- **Scrolling Capture (`L`)**: Capture entire chat logs, long webpages, or spreadsheets in one clean image — automatically removing scrollbars and sticky headers.
- **Screen Recording (`R`)**: Record any region as an **MP4 video (with system audio)** or **GIF**. When you hit stop, the file is automatically copied to your clipboard so you can paste and send it immediately.

### 6. Light, Beautiful, Private
- **Tiny ~10 MB installer**, silky-smooth Light & Dark themes with 4 accent colors, and 10 interface languages.
- Customize your toolbar, shortcuts, and optional floating edge ball to fit your exact workflow.
- **Privacy first**: Offline text recognition stays strictly on your PC, and your API keys are locked inside Windows Credential Manager — never stored in plain text.

## 5 Shortcuts to Master in 10 Seconds

*(Every shortcut can be customized in **Settings → Shortcuts**.)*

| Shortcut | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **`Shift+F1`** | Take a screenshot (annotate, read text, translate, scroll, or record) |
| **`Shift+F3`** | Pin whatever image or text is on your clipboard |
| **`Ctrl+Alt+D`** | Translate highlighted text anywhere |
| **`Ctrl+Shift+S`** | Snap & copy text immediately |
| **`Ctrl+Shift+O`** | Snap & translate immediately |
